          Privacy: no accounts, cookies, ads or tracking. Your stats and settings are saved only in this browser.

          How to play

          1. A legendary European Cup / Champions League line-up appears on the pitch — names hidden.
          2. Every shirt shows a real clue that suits the player's position: flag, age, shirt number, career goals, assists, caps or previous club. Tap a shirt to read it in full.
          3. Step 1: guess the club (3 tries; a yellow wrong guess means right country). Step 2: pick the season — the closer, the more points.
          4. Stuck? Team-wide clues add one stat to every shirt (all flags, all shirt numbers…). Bigger hints reveal the decade, a player, the manager or the result. All hints cost points.

          Classic — endless teams, build a streak.

          Time Attack — 60 seconds, club only, as many as you can.

          Difficulty (in settings): Easy 2 clues + kit colours (×0.75 points) · Medium 2 clues (×1) · Hard 1 clue (×1.5). Each team's rating also scales points: Easy ×0.8 · Medium ×1 · Hard ×1.3. Settings let you pick decades and a team pool (winners, runner-ups or famous clubs).
